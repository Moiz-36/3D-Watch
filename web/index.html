About this demo

This 3D model was made from one product photo using only free AI tools and open-source software (total cost €0). It shows the experience, not the final quality.

  • Strap: the shape, fabric texture and clasp are approximated. One photo doesn't show enough of the strap, so the AI had to guess most of it.
  • Back and caseback: not visible in the photo, so they are simplified.
  • Dial: a sharp flat photo, so the hands and sub-dials have no real depth.
  • Case details: lugs, crown and pushers are close but not exact.

How it can be much better: paid 3D tools, or the brand's own 3D files, combined with proper studio photos of each watch (a 360° turntable series plus close-ups of the dial, caseback and strap) would give an accurate model that looks right from every angle.
